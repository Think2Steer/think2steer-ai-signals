# AI Signals

AI Signals is a Think2Steer product that converts AI ecosystem changes into prioritized decision intelligence.

It helps teams understand what changed, why it matters, who should care, and what action may be needed.

## What It Does

- Ingests AI-related updates from selected external sources
- Enriches updates with structured business meaning
- Groups related updates into reusable signals
- Prioritizes signals by audience, impact area, and urgency
- Supports briefing, newsletter, dashboard, and export workflows
- Publishes structured signal events into the broader AI Impact Platform

## Product Problem

AI teams, product leaders, and executives are exposed to constant changes across models, vendors, research, regulation, tools, and platform capabilities.

Raw news is not enough. Teams need decision-ready interpretation:

- what changed
- why it matters
- which workflows or tools may be affected
- what action should be considered
- whether downstream evaluation is needed

## Platform Role

Within AI Impact Platform, AI Signals is the external change intelligence layer.

It can trigger downstream workflows such as:

- drift evaluation
- delivery impact assessment
- customer briefing generation
- product or governance review

## Example Use Cases

- Track AI model and vendor changes that may affect internal workflows
- Create executive-ready weekly AI change briefings
- Identify product areas affected by new AI capabilities or constraints
- Feed structured impact signals into release governance workflows
